# Code of conduct

This repository's issues and discussions are for making Filament better. Everyone who takes part is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, never attack people.
- Assume good faith, and stay on the topic.
- Keep it welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.
- Respect privacy: don't post anyone's personal information, and leave keys, tokens and private code out of logs and
  screenshots.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexual content, threats, and spam.

Comments that break these rules may be edited or removed, and the people who post them blocked. To report something,
email hello@filamentdev.com. Reports are read in confidence.
