Drive another machine's Filament from this window. On that machine, open Settings › Remote access, turn on Reach this machine on my network or Share on my tailnet, and make a code: paste its link here, or its address and the code. A machine you reach by SSH needs no code: type ssh:// and its Host. While this window holds its tunnel, the tunnel's port on this computer reaches that Filament for every program here, not this window alone.
